02 The tricks worth memorizing

Four small facts cover all fifteen major keys.

Order of sharps

FCGDAEB

Father Charles Goes Down And Ends Battle

Sharps are always added in this order. One sharp? It's F♯. Two? F♯ and C♯. And so on.

Order of flats

BEADGCF

Battle Ends And Down Goes Charles' Father

The exact reverse of the sharps. One flat? B♭. Two? B♭ and E♭.

Name a sharp key

Take the last sharp and go up one half-step — that's your key.

last sharp F♯ → G major
last sharp G♯ → A major

Name a flat key

The second-to-last flat is the name of the key.

B♭ E♭ A♭ D♭ → key of A♭
(F major, one flat, is the exception — just know it.)
