What the colors mean

♭
Amber = flat. The key lowers this note. Every B in F major sounds B♭.
♯
Blue = sharp. The key raises this note. Every F in D major sounds F♯.
♯
Reminder mark. Floats above notes the key alters that carry no written symbol — the ones easy to forget.
♮
Ink = play as written. Natural notes, and any note a written ♮ cancels back, stay in the default color.
